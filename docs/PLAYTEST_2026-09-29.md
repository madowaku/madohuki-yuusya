## Feel fixes made during testing

- Increased wipe radius to remove small corner remnants from ordinary zigzag strokes. The final 5.5% snaps clean rather than becoming pixel hunting.
- Kept single-ladder retrieval possible at either endpoint. Verified that missing ground-floor windows can be revisited after reaching the top.
- Queued approach-and-climb / approach-and-retrieve to remove unnecessary double presses when away from the ladder.
- Made every button at least 88×88 design pixels: 44×44 on a 360px-wide phone.
- Moved the flag out of title/HUD text, removed an unsupported decorative glyph, and checked Japanese and English layout.
- Added a brief clear-castle viewing interval before the result card, plus a warmer sky and sunrise.
- Ends a drag even when released over the HUD; pauses on focus loss; disables duplicate touch-to-mouse emulation.
- The final mouse pass measured 3.28–3.42 seconds per cleaning gesture. Its browser input overhead differs slightly from the touch pass.

## Source of diagnostic noise

The installed Godot skill's generic `run_scenario` helper currently reports enum-cast warnings inside its own `variant_codec.gd`. Its immediate process shutdown can also leave audio voices undrained. These are not accepted as game-validation results: the repository's standalone UI runtime suite exercises the same screens, explicitly stops/drains audio, preserves settings and exits with **zero diagnostics**. The generic helper's layout findings are retained separately.

## Puzzle and browser follow-up — 2026-09-30

The first 720×1280 mouse run exposed a real final-tower input bug: the selectable circles for the middle hooks and the top-floor hooks overlapped, so a tap intended for top hook 7 placed the ladder on hook 3. The fix moves the highest left/right hooks to x=112 and x=608 and widens their ledges to x=92–320 and x=400–628. Across 256 seeded layouts, each highest hook stays at least 88px from the same-side middle hook, matching two 44px selection radii. UI runtime checks now call the same screen-input handler and confirm both outer hooks place exactly once. The star-gazing moth remains the unique reward visible at F; the widened ledges and separated hook markers make the physical route readable without adding unrelated art.
